How to Remove an Image Background

1. Upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP image (up to 5 MB).
2. Click “Remove Background with AI”.
3. Wait while the AI model processes your photo.
4. Choose transparent or a solid/custom background.
5. Download your PNG image.

Note: On the first use, the AI model and runtime files are downloaded from IMG.LY's CDN. This download can be much larger than your uploaded photo and may take time. Later visits may use the browser cache.
